# Brand guidelines

The Falcon Times should feel **credible like a newspaper** and **friendly like a school community**:
editorial serif headlines, warm newsprint paper, navy and gold, and small touches of playfulness.
Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css` — change a value there and every page follows (including dark mode).

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Falcon Navy | `#002855` | Masthead, primary buttons, headers |
| Championship Gold | `#F2B01E` | Accents, badges, highlights, focus moments (never for small text on white) |
| Gold Ink | `#7A5200` | Gold-family text on light backgrounds (section labels) |
| Newsprint | `#FAF7F0` | Page background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
| Ink | `#131C2E` | Body text |
| Link Blue | `#14509E` | Links |

All text colours meet WCAG AA contrast on their backgrounds. A dark theme follows the visitor's system
setting (override with the Auto/Light/Dark switch in the top bar).

## Type

| Role | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masthead & headlines | **Playfair Display** | authority and heritage |
| Story text, decks, bios | **Source Serif 4** | comfortable long-form reading |
| Navigation, labels, timestamps, UI | **Inter** | crisp on phones and Chromebooks |

Fonts are bundled with the site (no Google Fonts request).

## Story layout

1. Section · sub-section label (gold, small caps)
2. Headline (Playfair, bold)
3. Deck (serif, one–two sentences)
4. Byline(s) → **Published <date at time zone>**; if edited: highlighted **Updated** notice with the change
5. Cover photo (original, high-res) with description, caption and *Photo: credit*
6. Body (drop-cap first paragraph, serif)
7. Tags, author card(s) with bio, feedback prompt (no comments), related stories

## Voice

Warm, clear and curious. Playful in headlines and polls, precise in facts. Whimsy lives in small details
(the "Daily Spark" quote ticker, the falcon crest), never at the expense of accuracy.

## Don'ts

* No unmoderated comment sections.
* No blurry screenshots, low-contrast text, or stock art standing in for reporting.
* No desktop-only layouts: everything must work on a phone.
