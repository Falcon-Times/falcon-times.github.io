Incorporated Best Practices
  • 6 distinct news sections with hover/tap sub-section dropdowns
  • Exact publication timestamps + transparent Update Logs
  • High-resolution photography with photo credits
  • Interactive student polls, cycling quotes & home/away scoreboard
  • Clickable Student Writer bio profiles
Strictly Avoided Pitfalls
  • No unmoderated public comment sections
  • No blurry phone screenshots or low-contrast text
  • No desktop-only fixed layouts (100% responsive mobile navigation)
  • Excluded internal "Layout & Design" from public news categories
